Black-Scholes Formulae
C = S·N(d₁) − K·e−rT·N(d₂)
P = K·e−rT·N(−d₂) − S·N(−d₁)
d₁ = [ln(S/K) + (r + σ²/2)·T] / (σ√T)
d₂ = d₁ − σ√T
S = spot  ·  K = strike  ·  T = time (years)  ·  r = risk-free  ·  σ = volatility

ℹ️ Methodology & Assumptions

📐 Pricing Model
Black-Scholes-Merton (1973) European option pricing model. Assumes log-normal returns, constant volatility (σ), and continuous risk-free rate. Greeks are computed analytically.
📡 Price Simulation
Nifty 50 spot price follows Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM) with configurable σ and r. A soft mean-reversion term keeps prices in a tradeable range. A real deployment would replace this with a live NSE/WebSocket data feed.
🎲 Market Prices
Simulated market prices add random bid-ask spread noise plus occasional structural mispricing (±3%) to mimic real options markets with liquidity gaps, order-book imbalances, and stale quotes.
🔍 Mispricing Detection
Mispricing = (Market Price − Model Price) / Model Price × 100%. Positive → market overpriced vs model (sell signal). Negative → market underpriced (buy signal). Thresholds: High ≥ 3%, Moderate ≥ 1.5%.
⚡ Update Frequency
Market ticks arrive every 100–2000 ms (configurable). The render loop uses requestAnimationFrame for smooth DOM updates, capped at 10 Hz to keep the table readable. True microsecond resolution would require server-side streaming.
📊 Implied Volatility
IV is back-solved from the market price using Newton-Raphson iteration. Converges in < 20 iterations for typical option parameter ranges. Displayed as annualised percentage.
